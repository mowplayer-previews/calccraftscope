How it works

Monthly payment = P × i ÷ (1 − (1 + i)^−n), where P is the loan amount, i the monthly rate (annual rate ÷ 12) and n the number of payments.

Early payments are mostly interest; later payments are mostly principal. The schedule below shows the full breakdown.

Frequently asked questions

Does this include fees or insurance?

No. It covers principal and interest only. Lenders may add fees, insurance or taxes.

What is APR?

APR includes some fees and is useful for comparing loans; this calculator uses the nominal annual rate you enter.
