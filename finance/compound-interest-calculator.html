How it works

Without contributions: Future value = P × (1 + r ÷ n)^(n × t), where r is the annual rate, n the compounding periods per year and t the years.

With monthly contributions, each deposit grows for the time it remains invested. The calculator works month by month using the equivalent monthly rate.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Rule of 72?

Divide 72 by the annual rate to estimate how many years it takes money to double. At 6%, about 12 years.

Are these results guaranteed?

No. Real returns vary; this shows the math for a constant rate. It is not financial advice.
