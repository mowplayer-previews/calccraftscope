How it works

X% of Y = Y × X ÷ 100. For example, 15% of 80 = 80 × 15 ÷ 100 = 12.

X is what % of Y = X ÷ Y × 100. For example, 12 is 15% of 80.

Percent change from A to B = (B − A) ÷ |A| × 100. A rise from 80 to 100 is +25%; a drop from 100 to 80 is −20%.

Frequently asked questions

Why is a 20% drop not undone by a 20% rise?

Because the second percentage applies to a smaller base. 100 − 20% = 80, and 80 + 20% = 96.

What is a percentage point?

The simple difference between two percentages: going from 4% to 5% is +1 percentage point, but a 25% increase.
