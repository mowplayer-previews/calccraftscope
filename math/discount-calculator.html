How it works

Sale price = Price × (1 − Discount ÷ 100).

Stacked discounts apply one after the other: a 20% discount followed by 10% is a total of 28%, not 30%.

Sales tax is added to the discounted price: Final = Sale price × (1 + Tax ÷ 100).

Frequently asked questions

Is “30% off then 20% off” the same as 50% off?

No. It works out to 44% off, because the second discount applies to the already reduced price.

Is tax calculated before or after the discount?

In most places, sales tax is charged on the discounted price.
