How it works

Direct: if A corresponds to B, then C corresponds to X = B × C ÷ A. Example: 3 kg cost $12, so 5 kg cost 12 × 5 ÷ 3 = $20.

Inverse: X = A × B ÷ C. Example: 4 workers take 6 days, so 8 workers take 4 × 6 ÷ 8 = 3 days.

Frequently asked questions

How do I know if it is direct or inverse?

If increasing one quantity increases the other, it is direct. If increasing one decreases the other, it is inverse.

Can I use it for recipes?

Yes. Scaling a recipe is a direct proportion.
