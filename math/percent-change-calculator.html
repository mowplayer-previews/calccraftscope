How it works

Percent change = (New − Old) ÷ |Old| × 100.

A positive result is an increase; a negative result is a decrease. The multiplier (New ÷ Old) tells you how many times larger the new value is.

Frequently asked questions

What if the old value is zero?

Percent change from zero is undefined, because you cannot divide by zero.

Is percent change the same as percent difference?

No. Percent difference compares two values to their average and has no direction; percent change measures movement from an old value to a new one.
