How it works

Tip = Bill × Tip% ÷ 100. Total = Bill + Tip. Per person = Total ÷ People.

Customs vary by country: in some places service is included in the bill, while in others a percentage tip is expected.

Frequently asked questions

Should I tip on the amount before or after tax?

Either is common; many people tip on the pre-tax amount. This calculator uses the amount you enter.

How do I round up?

Use the per-person amount as a guide and round to a convenient number.

Results are estimates provided for general information and may differ due to rounding.
