How it works

The calculator counts calendar days between the dates. Business days exclude Saturdays and Sundays (public holidays are not removed).

Years, months and days are counted following the calendar, like an age calculation.

Frequently asked questions

Should I include the end date?

Include it when counting days of an event that runs through the last day, such as a 3-day trip from Friday to Sunday.

Are holidays excluded?

No. Holidays vary by country, so only weekends are excluded from business days.
