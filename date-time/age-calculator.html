How it works

Age is counted in full years, then full months, then remaining days, following the calendar (months have different lengths).

Total days are the exact number of days between the two dates.

Frequently asked questions

How are leap-day birthdays handled?

For people born on February 29, the birthday in non-leap years is treated as March 1 by this calculator.

Is my data stored?

No. All calculations happen in your browser.

Results are estimates provided for general information and may differ due to rounding.
