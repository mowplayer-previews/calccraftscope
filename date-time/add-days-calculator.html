How it works

Days and weeks are added exactly. Months and years follow the calendar; if the target month is shorter (for example, adding one month to January 31), the result moves to the last day of that month.

Frequently asked questions

What is 90 days from today?

Leave the start date as today and enter 90 days.

Can I count business days only?

This tool counts calendar days. Use the days-between calculator to see business days in a range.
